DASHBOARD FLOW

Every line has a source.

This workspace follows the connected flow-map layout in your reference video. The flow lines show cumulative realized net P&L per stock from your saved backtests.

Green and rose identify profitable and losing stocks in the selected window. The ranking, distribution, cards and replay all use the same stock and direction filters. Setup rankings let you narrow the map further.

By default, Filtered selection keeps the summary, ledger and CSV export aligned with the map filters and replay. Choose Entire run to show the full run in the summary and ledger. Chart period buttons use the last 20 or 40 recorded sessions; each window begins at zero realized P&L. Drawdown is measured at session close.

Compare starts with dates recorded in all three latest versions. Full-history mode preserves each version's own calendar and shows coverage gaps explicitly.

This is historical research. Live quotes, sector classifications, order-book data and intraday candles are not present in these saved inputs and are not simulated.
