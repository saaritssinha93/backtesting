## Decision

The risk-sizing hypothesis does **not** demonstrate a profit improvement.
Keep G as the live incumbent. H remains research-only and every promotion gate
requiring future evidence or independent review remains blocked.

Stages G-0 and available-candidate G-3 are implemented. G-1/G-2/G-4/G-6 retain
the evidence limitations above; G-5 implements one fixed sizing experiment,
not all future strategy ideas. G-7 paired prospective H collection is not
implemented or scheduled. Future-data collection, exact ticks/fees and
independent review cannot be replaced by a historical backtest.

See `docs/V13_V10_H_BACKTEST.md` for commands, architecture, artifacts and
stage-by-stage limitations. Development runs named `20260927T...` were retained
for traceability; use the two explicitly named final runs above. In particular,
the first development run predates the float32 trigger normalization fix and
must not be used as final evidence.
