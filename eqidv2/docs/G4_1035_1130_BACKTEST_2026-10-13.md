## Method and evidence

The 49 sessions run from 29 July through 12 October 2026. October 1 is excluded
because immutable input was incomplete. The first 38 sessions, through
September 23, select thresholds. The next 11 sessions check those choices.
These dates have already been used in earlier research; the later period is
**not an untouched out-of-sample validation**.

The sweep evaluated 5,654 configurations over 1,043 candidates:

1. **Strict:** the original 09:50-style gates plus the original G4 tightening
   family, 18 variants per side.
2. **Relax 1m:** lower the confirmation body/volume minimums and increase the
   permissible directional wick, retaining the donor five-minute gates.
3. **Relax 5m:** also lower the five-minute price/volume thresholds, retaining
   the native source minimums and considering the one-minute combinations.

The source envelope always retains directional EMA9/20/50 order, increasing OI
of 0.05–1.00%, an absolute price move of at least 0.10%, five-minute volume
ratio of at least 0.80, and a directional confirmation closing beyond the
signal close. No relaxation goes below gates already applied to the sealed
historical source.

For each setup, selection stops at the first stage with at least five training
fills, at least 70% net wins, and positive net P&L. Within that stage, it chooses
the largest training net P&L, then lower daily drawdown. If no variant passes,
the setup remains visible as a failed diagnostic. Later outcomes never choose
these thresholds. Each stage is also compared independently using that stage's
training-eligible setups; those portfolios therefore have different memberships.

One highest-liquidity candidate is chosen per setup/session, before knowing
whether it fills. A missed trigger never selects a replacement. Both sides
use the same staged-stop simulator, including its conservative stop-first
handling of ambiguous bars. Capital is Rs 100,000 per entry with five-times
exposure and Rs 1,000,000 total capital. Net P&L deducts the existing five-basis-
point round-trip model. Broker-specific costs, added slippage and market impact
are not modeled. A win means positive net P&L, including profitable time exits;
it is not necessarily a target hit. Drawdown uses daily realized P&L.
