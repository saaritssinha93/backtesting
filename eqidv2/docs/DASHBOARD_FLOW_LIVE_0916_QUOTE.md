# Live 09:16 OI quote evidence

The isolated Dashboard Flow observer polls Kite full quotes for every stock in the exact dated near-month futures universe between 08:55 and 09:21 IST. It does not change the production collector, backtests, paper trading, or order placement. Each future session writes `dashboard_flow_observer/coverage/live_opening_oi_YYYY-MM-DD.json` below the configured data root. The JSON contains one row per dated-universe stock, including explicit missing rows and coverage counts.

A row is `QUOTE_NEAR_0916_NOT_CANDLE` only when its same-contract futures OI is positive, the Kite quote exchange timestamp is from **09:16:00 through 09:16:15 IST**, the observer received it **before 09:20:00**, and the quote age at receipt is at most 15 seconds. The row records `contract`, `oi`, `source=KITE_FULL_QUOTE`, `exchange_timestamp`, `received_at`, `seconds_after_0916`, `seconds_to_0920`, `quote_age_seconds`, `baseline_kind=PROVISIONAL_FULL_QUOTE`, and `exact_first_minute_candle=false`. The source interval to a 09:20 boundary is therefore the **actual quote time to 09:20**, not assumed to be exactly four or five minutes. A stock without such evidence remains `NO_QUALIFIED_QUOTE` after 09:20 with a reason; no value is filled from a later historical fetch or another contract.

This is a **provisional live quote baseline**, not the OI of the completed 09:15–09:16 one-minute candle. It cannot certify the exact historical first-minute value, and the observer alone cannot certify that the completed 09:20 futures bar arrived in time for a trade. Any future 09:20 setup must independently verify the completed bar's contract, source, exchange boundary, quality and receipt time, then state that its comparison uses a quote baseline. After-market historical candle recovery is valid for research but cannot be backdated into a live decision.

The collector's own status remains at `dashboard_flow_observer/status/opening_observer.json`. The coverage artifact reaches `FINAL` at 09:20 (or at collection completion), and a failed collection is marked `FAILED`. The raw timestamped quote events remain in `dashboard_flow_observer/raw/YYYY-MM-DD/events.jsonl` for audit.
