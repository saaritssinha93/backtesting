# G4 target sweep: 22 fixed entry setups

Tested 495 targets from **0.06% to 5.00%**, in 0.01% increments, for all 22
setups from the preceding 10:35–11:30 study: **10,890 setup/target trials**.
The 278 selected orders and their 245 potential fills remain fixed. Entry
filters were not selected again. Stops remain 1.25%, tightened to 1.00% after
120 minutes, with the same ten-minute trigger expiry and 15:15 square-off.

The objective is maximum positive net P&L subject to at least 70% positive-net-
P&L trades within the tested target grid. Each individual setup requires at
least five historical fills. Independent target choices optimize standalone
setup P&L; they are then replayed together under portfolio capital constraints.
This is not proof of a global optimum over every possible interacting target
combination. No selected historical target is at the 5.00% upper boundary.

## Training selection and chronological checks

The 49 sessions span 29 July through 12 October 2026. The first 38 select the
training targets, without using later outcomes. The next 11 check them. October
1 remains excluded for incomplete immutable input. The entry choices and these
dates were already studied previously, so this is not fresh out-of-sample data.

Four training-selected targets pass the minimum five training / three later
fills, positive P&L and 70% wins in both periods and overall. Their subset also
passes these conditions after portfolio allocation:

| Setup | Training-selected target | Training wins / fills | Later wins / fills | Total net P&L |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10:35 SHORT | 2.86% | 6 / 8 | 3 / 4 | Rs 32,393.71 |
| 11:00 SHORT | 0.12% | 9 / 9 | 3 / 3 | Rs 4,200.00 |
| 11:05 SHORT | 0.08% | 6 / 6 | 4 / 4 | Rs 1,500.00 |
| 11:25 SHORT | 1.41% | 4 / 5 | 6 / 8 | Rs 48,442.28 |

Together, these additions yield Rs 86,535.99 on 47 fills with 41 wins (87.23%).
Their combined G4 portfolio yields Rs 404,801.97 at 71.85% wins, with 72% wins
in the later period. Subset membership uses the later check, so that portfolio
is descriptive and must not be described as selected before the later period.
The historical 11:25 SHORT target is 4.20%; its training-only target is 1.41%.

All figures use Rs 100,000 capital per entry, five-times exposure, Rs 1,000,000
portfolio capital and five basis points round-trip cost. No additional market
impact or broker-specific costs are modeled. A target of 0.08% leaves only
0.03% after the modeled 0.05% cost; its high observed win rate has little cost
margin. Samples are small and many targets were tested. Historical optimization
does not establish a future 70% win rate.
