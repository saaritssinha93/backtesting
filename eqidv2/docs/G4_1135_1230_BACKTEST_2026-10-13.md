11:40, 11:45, 11:50, 11:55, 12:00, 12:05, 12:10, 12:15, 12:20, 12:25 and

three training-selected stage portfolios: Rs 868,179.53 combined with the

## Selection and execution rules

The study evaluates **6,168 configurations**: 209 LONG variants and 305 SHORT
variants at each of the 12 clocks. It tries stages in this order:

1. **Strict:** the original 09:50-style donor filters and G4 tightening family.
2. **Relax 1m:** loosen the exact next-minute confirmation's body, directional
   wick and volume filters while retaining the donor five-minute gates.
3. **Relax 5m:** also loosen the five-minute price/volume gates, within the
   verified source envelope, while considering the one-minute combinations.

For each setup, selection stops at the first stage with at least five training
fills, at least 70% net wins, and positive training net P&L. Within that stage,
maximum training net P&L wins, followed by lower daily drawdown, more fills and
a deterministic variant index. If no variant passes, the best available
training diagnostic remains visible; it is not labeled qualified. Later
outcomes do not choose the thresholds in these training-selected results.

The complete screen requires at least five training and three later fills,
at least 70% wins and positive net P&L in each period and overall, and the same
criteria after allocation with the baseline and all 24 new configurations.
The two qualifying setups are identified using that later screen; choosing
their membership is therefore descriptive and retrospective.

All relaxations retain directional EMA9/20/50 order, increasing OI between
0.05% and 1.00%, at least 0.10% absolute five-minute price movement, five-minute
volume ratio at least 0.80, and a directional confirmation closing beyond the
signal close. They cannot recover candidates excluded by the sealed source's
native gates. Confirmation always uses the **exact next completed minute**:
11:35 uses 11:36, for example. Relaxation concerns filters, not a shifted clock.

One highest-liquidity candidate is selected per setup/session before knowing
whether it fills; a missed trigger does not select a replacement. The trigger
expires after ten minutes. Square-off is 15:15. Both sides use the same staged
stop simulator and conservative stop-first treatment of ambiguous bars.

Sizing is **Rs 100,000 capital per entry, 5x exposure, Rs 1,000,000 total
portfolio capital**. Net P&L deducts **5 bps round-trip costs**, equivalent to
Rs 250 at Rs 500,000 exposure. Broker-specific fees, additional slippage and
market impact are not modeled. A win is positive net P&L, including profitable
time exits; it is not necessarily a target hit. Drawdown is based on daily
realized P&L.

## Other portfolio scenarios

The actual sequential strict-then-1m-then-5m selection includes **13
training-eligible new setups**. This differs from the independent stage
portfolios above: each setup stops at its first passing stage.
With the baseline it produces 302 wins in 420 fills (71.90%), Rs 831,248.26
full-period net P&L, but only 63.41% later wins. The additions alone produce

all-24 combined portfolio 364 wins in 536 fills (67.91%) and Rs 842,927.56.
Its later result is 69/111 wins (62.16%) and Rs 141,575.99. It fails the 70%
requirement. Adding all 24 is not justified by the complete screen.
