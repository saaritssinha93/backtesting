# G3 LIVE sizing update — 12 October 2026

The user selected **₹10,000 margin capital per trade with 5× target exposure**. Current G3 LIVE equity sizing therefore targets **₹50,000 per position**, replacing the previous one-share setting.

Quantity is `floor(50000 / signal_trigger_price)` whole equity shares. An operator quantity override can only reduce this number. A share above the budget produces zero quantity and blocks entry; non-equity lot sizes do not borrow this sizing policy. The broker still determines actual margin availability and order acceptance. This is planned exposure at the sizing reference; a different fill price can change actual exposure.

The LIVE execution policy has its own fingerprint. Current signals, LIVE orders, readiness evidence and arm records must bind to this policy as well as the strategy identity. Old one-share evidence cannot establish readiness for the larger budget. Importing the pure policy helper does not access a broker or grant execution authority.

Multi-share execution reconciles partial entry and exit fills, confirms cancellation before closing residual shares, and retains cumulative fills for weighted exit accounting. An uncertain broker response does not authorize a duplicate submission. Incompatible unsubmitted signals are isolated so they cannot interrupt protective management of already admitted positions. Dashboard P&L during partial exits stays unavailable where the reader cannot establish a complete combined value.
