## Selection decision

| Candidate | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `v17d selected` | chosen as default | Best balance of improvement quality: higher combined PnL, higher PF, materially lower drawdown, higher short PF, and materially lower short drawdown. |
| `v17d relaxed ADX scope` | rejected | Added a few more trades than the selected run, but gave back PF and PnL versus the selected profile. |
| `v17d aggressive RS` | rejected | Added the most trades, but short PF and combined PF both degraded too much versus `v17c`. |

## Bottom line

`v17d` does improve on the latest `v17c`, but the improvement came from making the short book cleaner rather than massively larger.

The strongest verified profile on full data was:

- a small trade-count lift,
- higher total PnL,
- higher short PF,
- higher combined PF,
- much lower short drawdown,
- much lower combined drawdown.

The more aggressive variants did increase trade count further, but once the short-side participation was pushed harder, PF and hit-rate started to deteriorate. Because of that, the `v17d` file was left on the stricter default profile.
