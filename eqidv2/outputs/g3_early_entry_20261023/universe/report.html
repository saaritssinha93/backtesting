Rules tested

Scan every 5 minutes from 09:20 to 13:30. OI is diagnostic, not a veto. Absolute 5-minute move at least 0.20%; volume ratio at least 0.40 or 0.80. Directional 1-minute confirmation: body at least 0.30, adverse wick no more than 0.60, volume ratio at least 0.50, close beyond both its open and the signal close. Aligned mode requires EMA9/20/50 in order. Turn mode requires EMA9 above EMA20 and price above EMA50 for longs, reversed for shorts. Opening Nifty opposition veto 0.05%. Breakout entries require a strict confirmation high/low break within 10 minutes; unfilled orders expire. Quotas 1, 2, or all per side, ranked by absolute move then liquidity. One executed trade per stock per day. Stop starts at 1.25%, tightens to 1% after 120 minutes; target 1.5%.

Execution and research limitations

  • Minute OHLC cannot establish crossing order among simultaneous triggers
  • Capital released at an exit minute-end is available to fills at the same timestamp
  • Pending signals reserve no capital; a trigger-time gate enforces the ten-position limit
  • Idealized fills, fixed 5bps costs, no additional latency/slippage or share rounding

Same-minute trigger ordering cannot be proven without ticks. In the 23 October broad-rule simulation, ADANIENSOL takes the last capital slot before DELHIVERY by deterministic rank. Trigger-time rejection requires a capacity-controlled execution engine, not submission of every pending stop order. These are 24 retrospective experiments, not an untouched validation set. No live or paper profile was changed.
