## C. ROOT CAUSE — corrected after empirical day-by-day check
**My first hypothesis (overlay leak) was WRONG — verified before any fix was written.**
- Non-conf setups (`MR_CONTROLLED_*`, `E_ORB_BREAKOUT_SHORT`, `D_EMA20_BOUNCE`) traded on
  **2026-06-16 ONLY** — the first conf-live day, before the conf scanner fully bootstrapped.
  06-17 onward and today the candidate + executed streams are **conf-only** (verified in
  `candidate_tickers_2026-06-22*.csv` and `signals_2026-06-22_*_id_5min_v7.csv`).
  → the conf gate (apply_conf_gate, scanner:1617) IS working; **no overlay fix needed.**
- **The real, ONGOING bleed is `P_PDH_BREAK_RETEST_LONG` — a CONF setup, correctly gated,
  that over-fires and loses every day:** 06-17 −3,883/8t, 06-18 −4,302/10t, 06-19 −4,263/13t,
  06-22 −2,049/13t. Its gate (`body≤0.75` mask + `premom_score≥75 & pre3_range≥0.50`,
  `missing_action: block`) IS enforced — it just **doesn't generalize** (overfit on tiny n),
  and the **0.5% SL / 0.6% target** scalp at ~13 trades/day is death-by-cost (PF 0.25).
- Same pattern, smaller scale: `L_RS_LEADER_VWAP_HOLD` (−6,619; mask-only, NO premom gate),
  `V_RECLAIM_PULLBACK_LONG` (−1,937; 0% win), `E_ORB_RETEST_HOLD_LONG` (−1,442; PF 0.01).

**So the fix is CONFIG, not wiring.** The bootstrap trades everything in
`FINAL_SETUP_CONF` (no per-setup enable flag); disable = move the setup to the separate
`RESEARCH_WATCH_CONF` block (`enabled: False`, never traded). Reversible. See `PROPOSED_FIX_2026-06-22.md`.
