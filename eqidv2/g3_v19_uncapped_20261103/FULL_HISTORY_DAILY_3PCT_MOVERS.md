**Definition:** For each stock, take the first available **09:16 IST 1-minute open**. Long move = (day's highest 1-minute high / that open - 1) x 100. Short move = (day's lowest 1-minute low / that open - 1) x 100. Rank each direction separately by absolute move, show up to five stocks reaching +3% or -3%, and break ties by symbol. The extreme time is the first minute when that price occurred. Close vs open uses the last available 15:15 minute close.

**Scope:** All stocks with 360 complete, validated 09:16-15:15 minute bars in each day's sealed G3 source; this is the G3 historical stock cohort, not every NSE-listed security. The 09:15 cash-opening minute is absent from this replay source. Early cached cohorts are historically selected and not strictly point-in-time universes. These are full-day observed extremes, not prices known at entry time or realized trading returns. A stock may appear in both directions if it crossed both thresholds.

**Threshold counts:** +3% on 63/63 days (948 stock-days); -3% on 63/63 days (1,041 stock-days).
