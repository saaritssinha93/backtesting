## Important publishing note

The live store remains the source of truth for price, stock, shipping, materials, and policy claims. Verify those details before publishing. In particular, do not use a blanket “waterproof” claim until it is supported for every advertised SKU. The claim-safe creative removes the inherited blanket waterproof footer.
