## Reel scripts

### Reel 1 — What does ₹599 get you?

Length: 12–16 seconds; vertical 9:16.

- 0–2 sec: sealed Trial Jar. On-screen text: `What does ₹599 get you?`
- 2–6 sec: quick opening and first reveal.
- 6–11 sec: show every included piece in natural light.
- 11–14 sec: show preference field or handwritten vibe card.
- 14–16 sec: `Tell us your vibe · Choose your jar`

Caption:

> A little surprise curated around your vibe 🎀 The Trial Mystery Jar includes 4–5 pieces, including one hair accessory. Example unboxing—contents vary. Comment “JAR” or tap the link in bio.

### Reel 2 — Three words to a jar

- Hook: `She gave us 3 words: gold, minimal, office.`
- Show the preference message.
- Film the selection process, one item at a time.
- Show the packed jar/box and bow.
- CTA: `Describe your style in 3 words.`

Caption:

> Your preferences guide the curation, subject to available styles. What three words describe you?

### Reel 3 — Proof and trust

- Hook: `Real photo. Natural light. No filter.`
- Show the exact SKU in window light, clasp/closure, scale against hand/ruler, and back side.
- Show accurate material/care card.
- CTA: `Save this before you choose.`

Use only substantiated material and care statements for that SKU.

### Reel 4 — Gift rescue

- Hook: `POV: you don’t know her exact style.`
- Choose recipient, budget, and vibe.
- Show gift-ready packing and optional note.
- CTA: `Send this to the confused gifter.`

Do not guarantee an arrival date beyond the published processing and delivery estimate.
