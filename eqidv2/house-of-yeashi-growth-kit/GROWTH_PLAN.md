# House of Yeashi Growth Plan

Audit date: 5 August 2026

## The strategic decision

Lead acquisition with the **personalised Mystery Jar** and use the regular jewellery catalogue for retargeting, cross-sell, and organic styling content.

Why:

- The offer is differentiated: the buyer chooses a tier and can describe a vibe, colours, or special request.
- A sampled Mystery Jar Reel had 36 likes and 10 comments, while a sampled regular product Reel had 11 likes and 2 comments. This is directional public evidence, not a substitute for Instagram Insights.
- The ₹599 Trial Jar is an accessible first purchase; the ₹1,199 Mini Jar clears the current ₹999 free-prepaid-shipping threshold and is the better AOV anchor.
- “Affordable anti-tarnish jewellery” is crowded. “The most personal, wearable surprise at an accessible price” is more ownable.

Primary promise:

> Trendy everyday jewellery, handpicked for your vibe—especially in gift-ready surprises.

## Baseline found in the live audit

- Shopify storefront with roughly 130 product pages, 13 collections, only 3 regular content pages, and no published blog articles.
- Instagram: 252 followers at audit time; recent posting is active. Mystery Jar content is the clearest engagement winner in the small public sample.
- Facebook: 44 followers at audit time and a numeric profile URL rather than a memorable vanity URL.
- Google ranks the homepage first when the exact spelling is forced, but initially corrects “Yeashi” to “Yashi.” This points to weak brand-entity authority and inconsistent off-site signals.
- Meta Pixel and Microsoft Clarity load on the storefront. No obvious GA4, GTM, or Google Ads ID was found in initial HTML; Shopify pixels can be sandboxed, so verify inside Shopify and the platforms.

## Fix before buying traffic

1. **Correct the phone number everywhere.** Contact/WhatsApp use `+91 9310232278`; the privacy policy shows `+91 93102322798`.
2. **Resolve shipping contradiction.** The homepage says free shipping on every Mystery Jar; the shipping policy says ₹100 below ₹999 and free prepaid shipping above ₹999. Decide the real rule, make checkout match, then mirror it in Merchant Center and ads.
3. **Publish and link Terms of Service.** The footer currently lacks it.
4. **Remove the unfinished gift-box text** “Next, add collections to display in the grid.”
5. **Create a SKU claim register.** The site mixes waterproof, water-resistant, and avoid-water instructions. Advertise only documented, SKU-specific claims.
6. **Correct product descriptions and identifiers.** Several products contain inaccurate or duplicate copy; normalize product type, brand, material, colour, SKU/MPN/GTIN, stock, and condition before feed sync.
7. **Move trust near Add to Cart.** Show processing time, delivery window, shipping charge, final-sale terms, damage-claim steps, care, and genuine reviews without hiding them in policy pages.
8. **Confirm trademark-sensitive product names.** Names referencing third-party marks such as Cartier or Swarovski should be used only when authentic and authorized; otherwise use generic design names.

Do not launch paid traffic until items 1–6 are complete and one test order validates tracking.

## Budget and economics

Do not choose CPA or ROAS from generic jewellery benchmarks.

Use:

`Maximum CAC = new-customer AOV × (pre-ad contribution margin % − desired post-ad contribution %)`

`Target ROAS = AOV ÷ maximum CAC`

Contribution margin must include product cost, discount, payment fee, packaging, subsidized shipping, failed-delivery cost, and expected replacement/refund cost.

Illustration only: at ₹1,199 AOV, 55% pre-ad contribution and 15% desired post-ad contribution, maximum CAC is about ₹480 and target ROAS is about 2.5.

Suggested media levels:

| Level | 30-day spend | Meta | Google | Use case |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Lean validation | ₹45,000–₹75,000 | ₹25,000–₹45,000 | ₹20,000–₹30,000 | One Meta Sales campaign + focused Google Search; add PMax after clean tracking and offer proof. |
| Full test | ₹90,000–₹150,000 | ₹50,000–₹90,000 | ₹40,000–₹60,000 | Meta prospecting + warm demand; Google Search + Merchant/PMax. |

If the available spend is below ₹45,000, avoid splitting across many campaigns. Run one Meta Sales campaign and tightly focused Google Search.

## Google plan

### Foundation and free reach

1. Connect Shopify’s Google & YouTube channel to Merchant Center, GA4, and Google Ads.
2. Sync the cleaned catalogue and enable free listings.
3. Submit `https://www.houseofyeashi.com/sitemap.xml` in Search Console and inspect the homepage, Mystery Jar, and top collections.
4. Correct all Merchant Center shipping/return mismatches and disapprovals before paid Shopping/PMax.

Google says Merchant Center free listings can surface products across Search, Shopping, Images, Lens, YouTube, Maps, and other eligible surfaces. Source: https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/13889434

### Search campaigns

Start with exact and phrase match:

- Brand: `house of yeashi`, `house of yeashi jewellery`.
- Mystery: `mystery jewellery jar`, `mystery jewellery box`, `jewellery surprise box`.
- Category: `trendy earrings online`, `dainty necklace online`, `everyday jewellery for women`.
- Benefit: `anti tarnish jewellery online`, and water-related keywords only for verified SKUs.
- Gifting: `jewellery gift for her`, `affordable jewellery gift`, `rakhi gift for sister` while seasonally relevant.

Initial negatives: free, DIY, making, wholesale, job, course, repair, real gold, diamond, jewellery-making supplies, second hand.

Keep brand Search separate so it does not inflate prospecting performance.

### Performance Max

Use after the feed, purchase tracking, and offer are clean. Keep the structure simple:

- Mystery Jars.
- High-margin bestsellers and ₹999+ bundles.
- Everyday jewellery and gifting.

Use Purchase as the primary bidding conversion; keep View Item, Add to Cart, and Begin Checkout as secondary diagnostics. Use real product imagery and original video. Google recommends a simple structure and sufficient learning time for retail PMax: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13776350

## Facebook and Instagram plan

Run Facebook and Instagram together in Meta Ads Manager.

### Core campaign

- Objective: Sales.
- Conversion location: Website.
- Optimization: Purchase/value.
- Geography: India as a hard control.
- Minimum age: 18.
- Audience suggestion: women roughly 18–44, while allowing the system to find gift buyers too.
- Placements: Advantage+ placements across Feed, Stories, and Reels.
- Exclusions: existing purchasers from new-customer acquisition when data quality permits.

At lean spend, use one ad set. At the full-test level, reserve 15–20% for warm demand only if the audience is large enough:

- Checkout/add-to-cart: 1–14 days.
- Product viewers: 1–30 days.
- Instagram/Facebook engagers: 30–90 days.

Meta’s current Reels guidance emphasizes 9:16 video, audio, captions/key messages in the safe zone, and testing: https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads

## Creative system

Launch with:

- Four 9:16 videos, 10–20 seconds each: ₹599 Jar unboxing, one piece/three looks, founder packing/curation, gift-selection scenario.
- Two 4:5 product/lifestyle images.
- One catalogue carousel.
- One dynamic catalogue ad after product IDs and Pixel/CAPI are validated.

Required Mystery Jar disclosure:

> Example unboxing. Contents vary by tier and curation.

Never fabricate reviews, scarcity, materials, plating, exact mystery contents, or durability.

## 30-day rollout

### Days 1–4: trust and economics

- Calculate contribution margin, AOV, and maximum CAC by offer.
- Fix phone, Terms, shipping, gift-page placeholder, claim inconsistencies, and inaccurate products.
- Add shipping/returns/care summary and genuine social proof near Add to Cart.

### Days 5–7: measurement and feed

- Connect Google & YouTube, Merchant Center, GA4, Google Ads, Search Console, and Meta catalogue.
- Validate Pixel + Conversions API, GA4 ecommerce, Google Ads Purchase, values, currency, transaction ID, product IDs, and deduplication.
- Place one real test order and reconcile Shopify, GA4, Google Ads, and Meta.

### Days 8–14: launch

- Launch one Meta Sales campaign and focused Google Search.
- Launch free listings; add PMax only when feed and purchase measurement are clean and budget supports learning.
- Test two angles: surprise/personal curation versus everyday styling/gifting.

### Days 15–21: diagnose

- Review Google search terms and negatives.
- Compare unboxing Reel versus 4:5 product/carousel.
- Read the funnel: impression → click → landing view → add-to-cart → checkout → purchase.
- Add warm retargeting only when the audience can deliver without excessive frequency.

### Days 22–30: scale carefully

- Test one landing-page variable: Mystery Jar page versus a curated ₹999+ bundle page.
- Keep the offer constant during the landing test.
- Pause a creative after roughly 1.5–2× maximum CAC with no purchase and weak downstream behaviour.
- Raise winning budgets only 10–20% at a time, approximately every 48 hours.
- Report by new-customer CAC, contribution ROAS, product, creative concept, placement, and audience.

## KPIs and decision rules

Commercial:

- New-customer CAC at or below maximum CAC.
- Contribution after advertising remains positive.
- AOV moving toward or above ₹999.
- New-customer revenue and first-order contribution.
- Failed-delivery, replacement, and refund rates.
- Blended MER: net store revenue ÷ total ad spend.

Provisional diagnostics, not promises:

- Meta outbound CTR: 1%+.
- Landing-page views/clicks: 80%+.
- Product-page add-to-cart rate: 4%+.
- Store purchase conversion rate: 1.2%+.
- Checkout-to-purchase: 35%+.
- Non-brand Google Search CTR: 4%+.

Interpretation:

- Strong clicks, weak add-to-cart: fix the offer or landing page.
- Healthy add-to-cart, weak purchase: inspect shipping, policy, trust, payment, and checkout.
- Platform ROAS strong but Shopify contribution weak: distrust platform attribution and reconcile transaction-level data.
