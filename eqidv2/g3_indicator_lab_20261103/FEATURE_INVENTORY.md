# G3 indicator inventory and testable hypotheses

Checked against source code on **2026-11-03**. Research scope: G3 V17 control, G7 V4 Lock comparison, saved V18/CVD experiments, and the isolated indicator lab through **2026-11-02**. This file inventories mechanisms and the frozen experiment definitions; it does **not** claim that a proposed indicator improves returns. Final numerical decisions belong to the lab's completed replay results. Existing history has been inspected and much of it helped choose the active slot policy.

## 1. What is actually in use

The full per-slot numbers remain in [FULL_SLOT_CONFIGURATION_2026-11-03.md](../g3_v18_20261102/FULL_SLOT_CONFIGURATION_2026-11-03.md). The important distinction is between compulsory evidence, economic filters, ranking, execution, and features calculated only for research. Removing an economic threshold must not turn unavailable evidence into a pass.

## 2. Source and clock definitions

Notation: `s=+1` LONG and `s=-1` SHORT; `O,H,L,C,V` are completed cash-equity candles; `Q` is OI of the identified dated stock future. Percent values below use percentage units: `0.30` means 0.30%, not 30%. Signal candles are five minutes and timestamped at candle end; confirmation is the **exact next completed minute**. A 09:20 signal has a 09:21 confirmation, followed by an eligible later trigger fill. Computing a feature from that completed minute cannot justify an earlier fill within the same minute.

**Two particularly important corrections:** native `prev_close=close.shift(1)` is the **prior completed 5m bar**, not the previous trading day's official/full-session cash close. A valid gap or prior-day-high/low feature must load the actual previous exchange session's cash data through its close; this strategy's 15:15 exit-truncated minutes are not sufficient to assert a 15:30 full-session close. Also, an OI change must compare positive valid values from the **same dated contract**. An expiry roll is not evidence of a sudden stock inflow/outflow.

## 3. Actual G3 V17 layers

### A. Data integrity and execution safeguards

| Safeguard | Actual behavior |
|---|---|
| Bar/time validity | Unique candidate per stock/signal, exact confirmation at signal+1m, exact 1m feature timestamp, 5m feature timestamp no later than signal and feature availability no later than confirmation. No gap-filled/opening-snapshot/stale/synthetic confirmation evidence. Five genuine source minutes per 5m bar where that evidence is declared. |
| Numeric validity | Positive finite OHLC with valid ranges; confirmation range must be nonzero; positive finite current and previous OI; finite changes, nonnegative valid volume ratios and traded value; body/wick ratios in [0,1]. Removing an OI threshold still requires actual valid OI. |
| Opening 09:20 evidence | Verified cash 09:15 open to 09:20 close price basis; actual 09:16-to-09:20 future OI evidence or the explicitly validated provisional-full-quote contract. Opening status must be `VALID`. No previous-session/fake first-candle fallback. |
| Confirmation direction | LONG confirmation closes above both its open and signal close; SHORT below both. Retained in this lab as a fixed setup safeguard, although its economic effect could be separately investigated later. |
| Early SHORT market veto | For signals 09:20 and 09:25, dated Nifty-future first-bar return must be at most -0.05%. Retained; an OR path does not bypass it. |
| Liquidity floor | Every row must meet its configured minimum traded value; current G3 values are zero, so finite/nonnegative checks do **not** establish genuine high liquidity. Native volume thresholds remain except in their named ablations. No bid/ask spread or order-book depth is available in these OHLCV files. |

### B. Eligibility filters

| Feature | Exact formula / rule | Warmup and missing behavior |
|---|---|---|
| EMA stack | `EMA_n = C.ewm(span=n, adjust=False).mean()`, n=9,20,50; `s*(EMA9-EMA20)>0 AND s*(EMA20-EMA50)>0`. Archived stack flags also checked where present. | Native EWM starts from the first loaded observation; it does not explicitly require 50 bars. Finite stack required in control; `no_ema` removes this economic gate. Track source history if changing seeding. |
| Signed move | `100*(C/previous_5m_C-1)` except verified opening basis; `s*move >= max(0.10, slot minimum)`. | Needs preceding actual 5m close or verified opening evidence. No zero/unknown side. |
| OI | `100*(Q/Q_previous-1)`; `Q>Q_previous>0`, change at least `max(0.05,slot minimum)` and at most 1%. | Two valid same-contract observations; opening uses special valid pair. Missing/stale/zero OI is not “falling OI.” |
| 5m volume ratio | `V / mean(previous 20 observed 5m V)`, current bar excluded; minimum 5 preceding observations; threshold `max(0.8,slot minimum)`. | Positive denominator; finite ratio. Native reference may span earlier sessions. It is not same-clock RVOL. |
| Candle body | `abs(confirm_C-confirm_O)/(confirm_H-confirm_L)` at least slot minimum. | Exact confirmation, nonzero range. |
| Adverse wick | LONG `(H-max(O,C))/(H-L)`; SHORT `(min(O,C)-L)/(H-L)` at most slot maximum. | Exact confirmation. 09:26 SHORT also requires lower wick at least 0.20 before quota. |
| 1m volume ratio | Confirmation V divided by mean of previous 20 observed completed 1m volumes, minimum 5 preceding observations. | Current confirmation excluded from denominator. | Extension | `s*(confirm_C-EMA20_1m)/ATR14_1m`, where ATR14 is the simple mean of 14 true ranges and TR=`max(H-L,abs(H-prev_C),abs(L-prev_C))`. | Existing 10:36 LONG/SHORT cap3; positive ATR and finite feature required where enabled. EWM20 seeding is native; ATR requires14 bars. No absolute-value transform: an opposite-side displacement is not a positive extension. |
| Slot allowlist | 34 enabled slot/side pairs from51 donor pairs; donor target/stop/price/OI/candle thresholds retained. | Selected using much of the same history through Oct27, including very small samples. This history is development evidence. |

`morning_slots=false`, `strict_0925_long=true`, `two_bar_continuation=false` in the resolved G3 policy; these are policy switches, not additional independent indicator votes.

### C. Candidate ranking and quotas

After all eligibility checks, the slot picker sorts descending by its configured `max_oi`=OI change, `max_volume`=5m volume ratio, `max_move`=absolute price change, `max_body`=body, or `max_liquidity`=traded value. Ties use traded value then symbol. Core candidates receive priority; eligible extras can fill remaining quota. Five currently meaningful core price thresholds are 09:26 SHORT .20 versus .13 eligible; 09:31 SHORT .20/.13; 09:36 SHORT .50/.325; 09:51 SHORT .20/.13; 11:21 SHORT .20/.13. Other resolved core/eligible equalities still follow native behavior. Quotas are one or two per slot/side, not unlimited capital assumptions.

Every lab ablation is applied **before** ranking/core classification/quota and allows replacement candidates. `no_core` removes priority, not the entry gates. A selected-trades-only edit cannot measure any of these changes faithfully.

### D. Position sizing and exits

Native G3 control uses ₹10 lakh capital, ₹1 lakh margin per position and 5x target exposure; nominal exposure ₹5 lakh per trade subject to actual allocation. Initial SL1.25%, tightened to1% after120 minutes; configured slot targets retained, full exit, close by15:15. Trigger order expires after10 minutes; minute OHLC ambiguity is resolved stop first. The runtime 90-second activation grace is a separate live-clock concept, not a historical ten-minute expiry replacement. No new partial exits, BE exit, daily stop or target tuning is introduced by this indicator lab. Costs are evaluated at5/9/15bps, with specified adverse-fill stress. These are friction assumptions, not measured capacity from actual depth.

### E. Calculated diagnostics

Signal/confirmation timestamps and flags, picker/core/pass reasons, stock and Nifty return from open, breadth coverage, relative strength, V18 VWAP/efficiency/pressure, lab same-clock count and EMA slope are available for audits or specified variants. Merely appearing in a feature parquet does not make a column an entry filter. MFE/MAE, final P&L and exit reason are **outcomes** and cannot enter pre-entry scoring.

## 6. What the current lab actually attempts

The frozen [protocol.json](protocol.json) contains **22 rule arms** including control and **2 model arms**. Ten one-change arms address EMA, OI lower/upper, 5m/1m volume, body, max/min wick, core priority and all51slots. Other arms compare three complete OR-path thresholds, fixed group ranking, OR+ranking, same-clock RVOL, extension2/3/4, efficiency≥.30 and opening breakout. Unfrozen ideas in the table above are not implied to have been tested.

The model pair is regularized logistic regression followed by shallow histogram gradient boosting, using the same pre-quota OR-eligible pool in34enabledslots. Training labels are native independently fillable candidate outcomes net5bps>0; unfilled orders have no win/loss label. Whole-day chronological blocks start with20trainingdays, then5testdays; minimum100filledlabels and15ofeachclass, otherwise explicitly flagged control fallback. Scaling/imputation/model fitting use earlier training data. LogisticC=.1; HistGB80iterations/7leaves/minleaf30/L2=1; fixedseed20261103. These are candidate ranking scores; calibration must be separately checked before calling them win probabilities. Same-day intraday labels ending by15:15 do not cross a whole-day boundary; any future overnight label would require purging/gap.

Selection alone cannot prove benefit. The smallest decision-changing evidence is a **paired native replay** of control versus one isolated change, including replacement orders, realized costs, daily loss amount, retained winners/new losers, and later chronological blocks. A new family should not be retained merely because in-sample win rate rises. Broad later-day improvement, neighboring-threshold consistency, cost/fill stress and future paper evidence are required before considering production. The protocol explicitly preserves live/paper configurations and existing result pointers.
